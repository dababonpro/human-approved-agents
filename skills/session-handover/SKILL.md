# Session handover

The next session knows only what was written down. Leave the facts it needs and nothing else.

## Record

One short line each, in the place your team keeps notes:

- What is now true
- What you verified, and how
- What is not done, or not verified
- Any blocker, and who it is waiting on
- The first next action

## Rules

- Write facts, not a transcript of files and commands. The history is already in version control.
- Use real dates, not "tomorrow" or "last week", because the note will outlive the session.
- Keep secrets and personal data out of notes.
- Mark anything unverified as unverified. The next session will trust what you write.
- Before acting on an old note, check the live system. Notes go out of date.
